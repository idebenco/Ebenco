Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is SmallScale and what do you offer? +

SmallScale is a leading wholesale foodstuffs supplier in Nigeria. We specialize in providing high-quality bulk food products to restaurants, retailers, food businesses, and institutions across Nigeria. Our product range includes grains, oils, spices, legumes, flour, and other essential food items at competitive wholesale prices.

Who can purchase from SmallScale? +

We primarily serve business customers including:

  • Restaurants and eateries
  • Retail stores and supermarkets
  • Hotels and hospitality businesses
  • Catering services
  • Schools and institutions
  • Food distributors and wholesalers
  • Other businesses requiring bulk food supplies
Do I need an account to place an order? +

While you can request a quote without an account, we recommend creating a free account for the best experience. Account benefits include order tracking, quick reordering, saved addresses, purchase history, and access to exclusive deals.

Ordering Process

  • WhatsApp: Message us at +234 800 000 0000
  • Email: Send your order to info@smallscale.com.ng
  • Visit: Come to our Lagos showroom during business hours

What is the minimum order quantity? +

Minimum order quantities vary by product. Generally, our minimums are designed for wholesale buyers. For specific MOQ information, please contact our sales team or check individual product pages. We're flexible and can accommodate different business sizes.

How long does it take to process an order? +

Order processing times depend on product availability and order size:

  • Standard orders: 1-2 business days
  • Large orders: 2-4 business days
  • Custom orders: 3-5 business days

We'll confirm availability and processing time when you place your order.

Can I modify or cancel my order? +

Yes, you can modify or cancel your order before it's processed. Contact us immediately via phone or WhatsApp. Once an order is processed and shipped, cancellation may not be possible, but we'll work with you to find a solution.

Products & Quality

What types of products do you sell? +

We offer a comprehensive range of wholesale foodstuffs including:

  • Grains & Cereals: Rice, corn, garri, and more
  • Oils & Fats: Vegetable oil, palm oil
  • Legumes: Beans, lentils
  • Flour & Baking: Wheat flour, semolina
  • Spices & Seasonings: Pepper, curry, salt
How do you ensure product quality? +

Quality is our top priority. We:

  • Source from verified and trusted suppliers only
  • Conduct thorough quality checks on all products
  • Store products in optimal conditions
  • Follow strict hygiene and safety standards
  • Provide 100% satisfaction guarantee
  • Replace any products that don't meet our quality standards
Are your products certified? +

Yes, all our products meet Nigerian food safety standards and regulations. We work with certified suppliers and can provide relevant documentation upon request.

What is the shelf life of your products? +

Shelf life varies by product type. We guarantee that all products are delivered with adequate shelf life remaining for business use. Specific shelf life information is available on product packaging and can be provided upon request.

Pricing & Discounts

How competitive are your prices? +

We offer highly competitive wholesale prices by leveraging our direct supplier relationships and efficient operations. We regularly review our prices to ensure they remain competitive in the Nigerian market.

Do you offer bulk discounts? +

Yes! We offer attractive bulk discounts based on order volume. The more you order, the better the price. Contact our sales team for a customized quote based on your specific requirements.

Are prices listed on your website? +

Due to market fluctuations and volume-based pricing, we provide custom quotes for each order. This ensures you get the most competitive pricing based on your specific needs and order size. Request a quote to get current pricing.

Do you offer special rates for regular customers? +

Yes! We value long-term partnerships and offer preferential rates to regular customers. Benefits include priority pricing, flexible payment terms, and dedicated account management.

Delivery & Shipping

Which areas do you deliver to? +

We deliver to all major cities and towns across Nigeria. Our primary coverage includes Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, and surrounding areas. For deliveries to other locations, please contact us to confirm availability.

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery times vary by location:

  • Lagos: 1-2 business days
  • Major cities (Abuja, PH, Ibadan): 2-4 business days
  • Other locations: 3-7 business days

We'll provide specific delivery estimates when you place your order.

What are your delivery charges? +

Delivery charges depend on location, order size, and weight. We offer free delivery for orders above a certain threshold in Lagos. Contact us for specific delivery costs to your location.

Can I track my delivery? +

Yes! Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive tracking information via SMS and email. You can also track your order through your account dashboard or contact our customer service team for updates.

Payment & Terms

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience:

  • Bank transfer
  • Online payment (card, USSD)
  • Cash on delivery (for approved customers)
  • Payment on credit (for verified businesses)
  • Mobile money (coming soon)
Do you offer payment on credit? +

Yes, we offer flexible credit terms to verified businesses with good track records. This requires business verification and approval. Contact our sales team to discuss credit options.

Is my payment information secure? +

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and work with certified payment processors to ensure all transactions are secure. We never store your complete card details on our servers.

Can I get an invoice for my purchase? +

Yes, we provide detailed invoices for all orders. Invoices are sent via email and can also be accessed through your account dashboard. We can customize invoices to meet your business accounting requirements.
