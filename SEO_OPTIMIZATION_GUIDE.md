## 17. Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

### DON'Ts

❌ **Keyword Stuffing**
Bad: "Wholesale foodstuffs Lagos wholesale food supplier Lagos wholesale food Lagos"
Good: "SmallScale is a leading wholesale foodstuffs supplier in Lagos, Nigeria."

❌ **Duplicate Content**
Don't copy product descriptions from other sites

❌ **Buying Backlinks**
Google will penalize - build organic links only

❌ **Ignoring Mobile**
80% of Nigerian traffic is mobile

❌ **Slow Loading Times**
Nigeria has slow internet - optimize for 3G

❌ **Neglecting Local SEO**
Local searches are crucial for wholesale business

❌ **Inconsistent NAP**
Business info must match everywhere online

❌ **No Reviews**
Reviews are critical for local SEO

❌ **Orphan Pages**
All pages must be linked from somewhere

❌ **Missing Alt Text**
All images need descriptive alt text

## 19. FAQ About SEO for Nigerian Businesses

**Q: How long until we see SEO results?**

A: Google dominates in Nigeria (95%+), but optimize for both.
